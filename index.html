Driver Diagram Tool

Create and edit a driver diagram with an aim, drivers, change ideas and measures. Save your work or download it as an image or PDF.

1. Save or open a diagram ▾

Save an editable copy of your diagram, or reopen a file that you saved earlier.

Save diagram creates an HTML file that you can open directly from a folder. CSV files are useful as backups and can be reopened using Open saved file.

AI-assisted diagram development (optional)

AI can help you explore or organise ideas for a driver diagram. It is optional: the diagram should always reflect your improvement group's own understanding and decisions.

Important: protect confidential information

Do not paste or upload patient-identifiable, staff-identifiable, other personal or confidential information into an AI chatbot unless your organisation has specifically authorised that AI service and that use of the information.

Meeting notes, workshop findings, incident reports and slides can identify people indirectly, even when names have been removed. Check documents carefully before sharing them.

Access to an organisation-provided AI product, including Microsoft 365 Copilot, does not automatically authorise every type of data or every use case. Follow your local IG and AI policies. If unsure, ask your IG lead, Data Protection Officer or Caldicott Guardian.

What this tool does: This panel provides guidance only. It does not connect to an AI service, accept project documents or send diagram information to a chatbot.

Three ways AI could help

Starter for 10

Create a deliberately provisional draft from project documents or background context. Expect the improvement group to make substantial changes. Avoid suggested measures unless requested.

Evidence-informed synthesis

Organise an existing fishbone diagram, engagement findings or workshop outputs into a proposed theory of change. Preserve the team's language, and make disagreement or uncertainty visible.

Speculative exploration

Explore plausible drivers and possible changes for a complex objective. Use the result to identify questions and people to involve, not as an agreed plan for implementation.

Before using any AI-generated diagram: Review the wording, relationships, evidence and proposed changes with your improvement group. A polished diagram is not proof of an agreed or valid theory of change.

        Help

        About this tool

        Use this tool to create and update a driver diagram containing an aim, primary drivers, secondary drivers, change ideas and measures.

        The diagram updates as you add, edit, move, connect or colour items. You can save an editable copy of your work or download the finished diagram as an image or PDF.

        1. Understanding the diagram

        • Aim: the overall outcome you want to achieve.
        • Primary drivers: the main factors that need to be in place to achieve the aim.
        • Secondary drivers: the more detailed factors that influence the primary drivers.
        • Change ideas: practical actions or tests of change that may influence the drivers.

        The diagram shows how change ideas contribute to secondary and primary drivers, which together support the overall aim.

        2. Adding items

        • Click the + button on a box to create an item at the next level. It appears connected to its parent automatically.
        • Type directly into the new box, then click ✓ to confirm or × to discard it. You can also use Ctrl+Enter to confirm or Escape to cancel.
        • New items inherit their parent's colour. Use the palette button while editing to choose a different colour.

        3. Editing and organising items

        • Click a box once to select it. Click the pencil icon or double-click the box to edit its text directly.
        • Click ✓ to save an edit or × to cancel it. Ctrl+Enter and Escape work too.
        • Drag a driver or change idea to reorder it among items with the same parent. You cannot drag it into a different branch.
        • Click a column heading to rename it. Boxes expand to fit longer text by default.
        • Use Undo and Redo at the top of the page to reverse or restore changes. The right-click menu remains available for additional actions.

        4. Adding and managing measures

        • Use Add measure beneath a box to add a measure. The control appears when you hover over the box or its measures.
        • Choose an outcome, process or balancing measure, or leave the type unspecified. Measures appear beneath their related box.
        • Click a measure to select it, or double-click to edit it. Drag measures up or down to reorder them within the same box.
        • You can also use Alt+Up or Alt+Down to reorder a focused measure. The measures key explains the coloured bullets.

        5. Colours and key

        • Open Colours and key in the editing panel to add, rename or remove named colours.
        • While editing a box, click the palette icon at the bottom-left to choose an existing colour, remove its colour or create a new named colour.
        • The selected colour is previewed immediately. Click ✓ to confirm the text and colour together, or × to cancel both changes.
        • Colours can show themes, workstreams, teams or priorities. The key updates automatically when changes are confirmed.
        • Use Hide key or Show key to control whether the key appears.

        6. Diagram style

        • Open Diagram style in the editing panel.
        • You can adjust box height, spacing, font size, font family and bold text.
        • These settings change how the diagram looks but do not change its content or structure.
        • Auto-sized boxes may grow beyond the standard box height when more space is needed.

        7. Connections

        • Items are connected to their parent automatically when they are created.
        • Use the connector control on a box to add or remove an additional connection.
        • Additional connections can be used when an item contributes to more than one driver.

        8. Deleting or starting again

        • Right-click an item and choose Delete to remove it.
        • Deleting a parent item may also remove its dependent child items.
        • Connections linked to deleted items are removed automatically.
        • Use Start new diagram to clear the current diagram. Save your work first if you may need it again.

        9. Saving your work

        Open Save or open a diagram in the editing panel. Three options are available:

        • Save diagram: creates an editable HTML file containing both the tool and your diagram. Double-click the saved file to open it directly in a web browser.
        • Open saved file: opens a CSV backup that was previously downloaded from the tool.
        • Download CSV backup: creates a separate data backup that can be imported into the tool later.

        When you make changes in a portable HTML file, use Save diagram again to create an updated copy. The browser will not normally overwrite the original file automatically.

        10. Downloading the finished diagram

        • Use Download image to create a PNG image for presentations, reports or documents.
        • Use Download PDF to create a PDF version for sharing or printing.
        • Editing controls are hidden automatically from downloaded images and PDFs.
        • The diagram, measures and key are included in the downloaded output when they are visible.

        11. Screen controls

        • Hide editing panel: gives the diagram more room on the screen.
        • Show editing panel: brings the editing options back.
        • Hide key: removes the colour and measures key from view and from the downloaded output.
        • Help: opens this guidance.

        12. Using Diagram and Outline views

        • Use Diagram and Outline to switch between the visual diagram and a hierarchical list of the same information.
        • In Outline, expand a branch using its arrow. Select an element's text to locate its box in Diagram view.
        • Select Edit to change text or + Add child to create the next level. Save or Cancel your changes before switching views.
        • Open Actions to add a measure, change a colour, delete an element, or move an element up or down among siblings with the same parent.
        • Use a measure's Edit button to update or delete it. Undo and Redo also work for changes made in Outline.
        • On smaller screens, Outline is designed for touch and keyboard use. Diagram view keeps four columns and can be scrolled sideways.
        • To download PNG or PDF, the tool uses the visual Diagram view. Save editable HTML or CSV backups to keep working on your diagram.

        AI-assisted diagram development (optional)

        Expand AI-assisted diagram development in the editing panel to explore three possible uses of AI and read the information- governance warning. This guidance does not contact an AI service.

        Use only an AI service authorised by your organisation for the intended information and task. Review any AI-generated theory of change with the improvement group; the group retains ownership of the diagram.

        Information governance

        Saved HTML and CSV files contain the text and data entered into the diagram. Store and share these files in line with your organisation’s information-governance and cybersecurity policies.

        Do not include patient-identifiable or other sensitive personal information unless its use and storage have been appropriately approved.
